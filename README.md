# Derek Wood  



Product designer / design engineer / teacher.

I started my career as a front-end developer. Over time, clients stopped asking me only to build things and started asking me to help figure out what should be built.

(note: most of my early code was on BitBucket and GitLab)

I work across research, product strategy, information architecture, visual design, design systems, prototyping, and front-end development. Sometimes the fastest way to answer a design question is Figma. Sometimes it's code. Increasingly it's an agentic workflow. I care less about which tool we're using than whether we're actually learning something.

I've spent a lot of my career in the space between design and development. I like turning visual systems into working systems, helping designers understand the medium they're designing for, and helping developers understand the design decisions underneath the interface.

## Perpetual Education

In 2019, I started researching what a genuinely cross-disciplinary product design education might look like. That eventually became Perpetual Education.

Over the next several years I designed the curriculum, platform, progression model, and supporting systems; ran overlapping cohorts; managed TAs and interns; taught designers and developers; and continuously changed the program based on what we learned.

The curriculum covers the whole process: typography, visual design, information architecture, accessibility, UX, programming, design systems, product strategy, and how those things actually work together.

After four years of cohorts, we redesigned the program around self-paced learning. It now runs independently, and I stay involved as an advisor.

A lot of the ideas and tools we developed along the way live here on GitHub.
